What is our system?

One simple platform for academic
and career decisions.

The Student Performance & Career Guidance System combines academic performance analysis, career assessment, and career prediction in one platform.

Students can view subject-wise marks, attendance, semester performance, percentages, charts, career results, and useful reports — all from their own account.

The system uses a student's academic information and assessment answers to provide understandable career recommendations and guidance.

Academic performance View marks, attendance, subjects, grades, and progress.
Career direction Explore suitable career options and recommended skills.
Useful reports Download performance and career reports in PDF or Excel.
How it works for students

From login to a clearer next step.

The system follows a simple process so students can understand their current position and plan what to do next.

01

Register / Login

Students create an account and securely log into their personal student portal.

02

Complete Profile

Students provide basic academic, skill, interest, and study-related information.

03

View Academic Performance

Students view marks, attendance, subjects, semester performance, percentage, and grades.

04

Performance Analytics

The system presents charts, trends, strong subjects, and areas that need improvement.

05

Career Assessment

Students answer questions about interests, skills, academic preferences, and study habits.

06

Career Prediction

The system compares student information with career criteria and calculates suitable recommendations.

07

Career Guidance

Students receive recommended career paths, required skills, and areas they can improve.

08

Reports

Students can generate and download performance, analytics, and career reports in PDF or Excel format.
