What this module does

Career guidance that starts with your own information.

The system does not make a random prediction. It considers your marks, strong subjects, interests, and assessment answers.

Career assessment

Answer simple multiple-choice questions about programming, mathematics, databases, networking, data, design, communication, and management.

System recommendation

The rule-based system combines questionnaire scores and academic performance to calculate a suitable career recommendation and match percentage.

Practical direction

See recommended skills, alternative career options, and a separate roadmap for the career you personally want to pursue.

Recommendation vs personal goal

Two career features, one clearer future.

Career Guidance

  • Uses marks, strong subjects, and questionnaire answers.
  • Shows a recommended career and match percentage.
  • Explains why the recommendation may suit you.
  • The assessment can be completed only once.

My Career Path

  • You personally select the career you want to pursue.
  • Your chosen career is not changed by the recommendation.
  • Follow a skill roadmap made for your selected career.
  • Mark skills as Not Started, Learning, or Completed.
How Career Guidance works

Four steps from interest to career direction.

01

Complete the test

Answer ten questions honestly about your interests, skills, and preferences.

02

System checks your data

Your answers are combined with marks, percentage, and strong academic subjects.

03

See your recommendation

Receive a primary career, match percentage, reasons, skills, and alternatives.

04

Choose your path

Select the career you want and track the skills needed to become career ready.
