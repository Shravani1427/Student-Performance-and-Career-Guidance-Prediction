What this module does

Your academic information, made easy to understand.

Instead of checking separate records, students can see their complete academic position in one personal dashboard.

View academic records

See subject-wise marks, total marks, obtained marks, internal marks, practical marks, assignments, semesters, and grades.

See visual analytics

Use bar charts and doughnut charts to understand subject performance, overall percentage, attendance, and mark distribution.

Find improvement areas

The report card highlights strong subjects, weaker subjects, pass or fail status, grades, and simple improvement tips.

What students can learn

Use your results to plan your next study step.

Performance insights

  • Understand your overall percentage and performance level.
  • Identify your highest-scoring and lowest-scoring subjects.
  • Compare subject marks through simple charts.
  • Check whether your attendance is on track.

Personalized action

  • Spend more revision time on subjects below your target.
  • Keep building on subjects where you are strong.
  • Use the report card tips to create a study plan.
  • Track the next semester against your current results.
